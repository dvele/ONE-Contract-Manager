AZ-1. Notice of Right to File a Complaint with the Registrar of Contractors (A.R.S. § 12-1365)

Under Arizona Revised Statutes section 32-1155, a buyer of a dwelling has the right to file a written complaint against the homebuilder with the Arizona registrar of contractors within two years after the close of escrow or actual occupancy, whichever occurs first, for the commission of an act in violation of Arizona Revised Statutes section 32-1154, subsection A.

AZ-3. Escrow Disclosure (A.R.S. § 41-4030)

Buyer may request that the Agreement Deposit and the other payments under the applicable Exhibit A be held in an independent escrow account with an escrow agent licensed in Arizona until the sale is consummated or the applicable Exhibit A is terminated. Unless Buyer requests an independent escrow account before signing the applicable Exhibit A, those payments will be handled through Dvele's trust account as permitted by A.R.S. § 41-4030(C).

AZ-4. Purchaser Dwelling Actions (A.R.S. §§ 12-1361 to 12-1366)

Before bringing a dwelling action, Buyer must give written notice of the alleged construction defects and allow the opportunity to inspect and to repair or replace, as provided in A.R.S. § 12-1363. The notice-and-repair process in A.R.S. § 12-1363 and the warranty claim process in Exhibit E run before the dispute resolution procedures in Section 12 of the Agreement, and nothing in Section 12 or Exhibit E shortens any period that A.R.S. § 12-1363 extends.

AZ-5. Owner-Occupant Lien Protection (A.R.S. § 33-1002)

If Buyer is an owner-occupant as defined in A.R.S. § 33-1002, no lien may be recorded against Buyer's dwelling for labor, materials or services except by a person having a direct written contract with Buyer. Nothing in Section 4(c) of the Agreement waives that protection.

AZ-6. Installation by Buyer (FOB Factory)

If Buyer takes delivery of the Home at the factory, installation of the Home in Arizona requires an installation permit from the Arizona Department of Housing (A.R.S. § 41-4004(E)) and must be performed by a licensed contractor. Buyer may request the name and license number of any contractor engaged to install the Home.

AZ-7. Solar Energy Device Warranty (A.R.S. § 44-1762)

If the Home is sold with a solar energy device, the collectors, heat exchangers and storage units of the device and its installation are warranted for at least two years, and the remaining components for at least one year, as required by A.R.S. § 44-1762. This statutory warranty applies notwithstanding Section 6 of Exhibit E, and Dvele will furnish Buyer the written warranty statement and the certificate of compliance required by A.R.S. § 44-1762(B) and (C).
