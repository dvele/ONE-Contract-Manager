C.3 Site Readiness Requirements (Pre-Delivery)

If FOB Factory is elected in Exhibit A, this Exhibit C.3 reflects Buyer/GC’s own transport and installation requirements at the Site; Dvele has no delivery, site-readiness verification, or inspection obligations, and any confirmations below run to Buyer/GC’s own contractors, not to Dvele.

Buyer/GC must provide written confirmation (and photos where requested) that:

  • Foundation meets plan tolerances; anchor points installed as specified
  • Utility stubs placed and tested where required
  • Access routes and crane staging are ready; road load limits confirmed
  • Permits/inspections for set are scheduled/available
  • Site is safe, secured, and free of standing water / debris

If the site is not ready, Dvele may delay delivery and costs apply per Exhibit A.

C.6 Indemnity for Non-Dvele Work (Market-Norm)

Buyer shall defend and indemnify Dvele from third-party claims arising from Buyer/GC site work, safety practices, or acts/omissions of Buyer or its contractors; the transport, storage, installation, use, or operation of any Home after risk of loss passes to Buyer under Section 4 (excluding work Dvele performs as GC); any sale, lease, or other transfer of a Home by Buyer; or any claim by a subsequent owner, tenant, or occupant to the extent it exceeds Dvele’s obligations under Exhibit E, except to the extent caused by Dvele’s negligence or willful misconduct.
